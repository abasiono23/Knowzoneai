Questions about plans

What are AI credits?

Credits pay for big AI jobs. A study pack from your notes costs 1, and a video explainer costs 1 to 5. Unused credits don’t expire.

Can I cancel a paid plan?

Yes, any time. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. Your first payment is fully refundable within 7 days.

Is Knowzone free?

Yes. The core of Knowzone is free for everyone: 10 AI tutor questions a day, study tools, videos and games. Paid plans add more AI for students who want it, never the basics.
