Why we’re building it

Studying shouldn’t feel lonely, or confusing.

Millions of students prepare for JAMB, WAEC, NECO and university exams with crowded classrooms, expensive lessons and a textbook that only explains things one way. When you’re stuck at 10 pm, there’s often nobody to ask.

Knowzone changes that. Kora, our AI tutor, explains anything step by step, as many times as you need, in your language. Your notes become flashcards and quizzes in a minute. Practice papers show you what exam day feels like. And when studying gets hard, your friends are one Study Room away.
