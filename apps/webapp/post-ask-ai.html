How to learn with AI, not just copy from it

AI can be a patient tutor or an easy way to learn nothing. The difference is how you ask.

Using AI · The Knowzone team

An AI tutor is there whenever you’re stuck. But if it does all the thinking, you won’t be able to do it alone in the exam. Here’s how to make it work for you.

Try first, then ask

Have a go at the question, then show the AI your work and ask where you went wrong.

Ask for steps, not only answers

Ask it to explain each step and why it works. Then cover the answer and do a similar question yourself.

Ask it to test you

Say “ask me five questions on this topic, one at a time”. It’s one of the best ways to revise.

Check what matters

AI can be wrong. Check important facts against your textbook or teacher, especially dates, formulas and definitions.
