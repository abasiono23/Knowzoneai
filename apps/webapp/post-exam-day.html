How to manage your time in an exam

A simple plan for reading the paper, sharing your time and not getting stuck on one question.

Exam skills · The Knowzone team

Good students lose marks when they run out of time, not only when they don’t know the answer. A plan you practise beforehand helps you stay calm.

Share out your time first

Divide the time by the marks. If a paper has 100 marks in 2 hours, that’s about one minute for each mark, with a little left over to check your work.

Answer what you know first

Go through the paper and collect the easy marks. It builds confidence, and it protects those marks if time runs short.

Don’t get stuck

Mark a hard question, move on and come back to it. A fresh look often helps.

Keep time to check

Use your last few minutes to look for slips: a missed question, a wrong sign, or an answer in the wrong place.
