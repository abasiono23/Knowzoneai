Privacy

Waitlist privacy notice

Short and plain: what we collect when you join the waitlist, and why.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Whether you’re a student, parent, teacher or school, what you’re preparing for, and your country, if you tell us.
  • Where you heard about us, if you came from a shared link.

Why we collect it

To tell you when Knowzone launches and send you launch news, and to understand who is waiting for Knowzone so we build the right things first. We send one confirmation email when you join.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We don’t email you about anything other than Knowzone.

How long we keep it

Until Knowzone launches and we’ve invited you in, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever comes first.

Leaving the list

Reply to the confirmation email and ask to be removed, and we’ll delete your details.

Where it’s stored

Your details are stored securely in Knowzone’s database and are only used by the Knowzone team.

Last updated: October 2026. The Knowzone app has its own privacy policy, shown in the app before you sign up.
