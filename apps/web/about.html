About Knowzone

Every student deserves a great teacher, any time.

Knowzone puts an AI tutor, real exam practice and a community of learners in every student’s pocket, so where you live or what you can afford never decides how far you go.

Why we’re building it

Studying shouldn’t feel lonely, or confusing.

Millions of students prepare for JAMB, WAEC, NECO and university exams with crowded classrooms, expensive lessons and a textbook that only explains things one way. When you’re stuck at 10 pm, there’s often nobody to ask.

Knowzone changes that. Kora, our AI tutor, explains anything step by step, as many times as you need, in your language. Your notes become flashcards and quizzes in a minute. Practice papers show you exactly what exam day feels like. And when studying gets hard, your friends are one Study Room away.

What we believe

Our principles

🎯

Accurate before clever

Learning content is checked by a second, independent review before students see it, and Kora says so when it isn’t sure. A wrong answer is worse than none.

🌍

Made for here, open to everyone

Built around African classrooms and exams, in ten languages from Yorùbá to Arabic, and useful to any learner, anywhere.

🎮

Learning should be fun

Streaks, quests, quiz battles and whole game worlds, because you learn best when you actually want to keep going.

🛡️

Safe by design

Messages and comments are checked before anyone sees them. Reporting and blocking are one tap away, and you decide who sees your profile.

🔒

Your data is yours

We don’t sell your data. You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, from the app’s settings.

💚

Free to start

The core of Knowzone is free for everyone. Paid plans add more AI for students who want it, never the basics.
