FAQ

Questions, answered

When does Knowzone launch?
We’re putting the finishing touches on the Android app now. Join the waitlist and you’ll be among the first to know the moment it opens.
Is Knowzone free?
Yes. The free plan includes the AI tutor (10 questions a day), study packs from your notes, video lessons, fun-fact reels, Knowzone World, Study Rooms and sample exam papers. Plus and Pro add more AI and every practice paper.
Which exams does it cover?
JAMB UTME, WAEC WASSCE and NECO SSCE, in ten subjects: English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Government, Literature, Geography and Commerce. The practice papers follow each exam’s real format and timing, and every answer is explained. Courses, the AI tutor and study packs work for university and any other subject too.
Are the practice papers real past questions?
They’re practice papers written in each exam’s exact format and checked by a second, independent review for accuracy. We’re adding licensed past papers from previous years as well.
Who is Knowzone for?
Secondary-school students preparing for exams, university students, and anyone who wants to learn something new. Parents and teachers are welcome on the waitlist too.
Is it safe for young learners?
Chat messages and comments are checked before anyone sees them, anyone can report or block, and you control who sees your profile. Study Rooms are private unless you open them to your school.
Do I need the internet?
Yes, for most of the app: the AI tutor, videos and Study Rooms all work online.
